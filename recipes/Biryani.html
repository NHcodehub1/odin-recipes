Biryani

Description

Biryani is a mixed rice dish traditionally made with rice, meat (chicken, goat, beef), seafood (prawns or fish), or vegetables, and spices.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken, meat, or vegetables
  • Yogurt
  • Sliced onions
  • Ghee or oil
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Fresh mint and coriander
  • Green chillies
  • Whole spices (bay leaves, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves)
  • Ground spices (biryani masala, chilli powder, turmeric)
  • Saffron or turmeric milk
  • Lemon juice

Steps

  1. Marinate the protein in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and ground spices for 30 minutes.
  2. Wash and soak the long-grain basmati rice in water for 30 minutes.
  3. Fry sliced onions in ghee or oil until they turn golden brown.
  4. Remove half the fried onions for later, then add the marinated protein to the pan.
  5. Cook the protein until it is completely tender and the gravy is thick.
  6. Boil water with whole spices and cook the rice until it is 70% done.
  7. Drain the rice and layer it directly over the cooked protein gravy.
  8. Top the rice layer with the reserved fried onions, mint, and coriander.
  9. Drizzle saffron milk, lemon juice, and a spoonful of ghee over the top.
  10. Seal the pot tightly with a lid or dough to trap the steam.
  11. Cook on very low heat for 15 to 20 minutes using the dum method.
  12. Fluff the rice gently from the sides before serving the biryani hot.
